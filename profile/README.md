## The challenge

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**The system is changing**

Electric heat and transport are creating new demand patterns on networks designed for a different load profile.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**Peaks are harder to predict**

Demand varies with weather, behaviour, building characteristics and the mix of low-carbon technologies.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**Constraints are local**

The useful response depends on the feeder, street or neighbourhood where spare capacity is disappearing.

</td>
</tr>
</table>

<br>

Heat pumps and EVs are changing when and where electricity is used. The hardest constraints
often occur at the low-voltage edge, where visibility is weakest.

## Field trials

We are looking for partners with a defined low-voltage network challenge, relevant building
or device data, and a clear outcome to test.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="25%" valign="top"><b>Defined local area</b><br><br>A feeder, street, neighbourhood or comparable network boundary.</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top"><b>Relevant assets</b><br><br>Heat pumps, EVs or other loads with measurable flexibility.</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top"><b>Minimum useful data</b><br><br>Enough network and building context to test the hypothesis.</td>
<td width="25%" valign="top"><b>Shared success measure</b><br><br>A technical or operational outcome agreed before the trial.</td>
</tr>
</table>
